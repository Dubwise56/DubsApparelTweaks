# Dubs Apparel Tweaks

By Dubwise

Equip apparel quickly, hide hats indoors, show hair under hats.

Everything is in the mod options, and nothing is stored in your save, so it is safe to add or remove at any time.

## Equip speed

- Wearing and removing apparel takes as long as you want instead of the vanilla duration.
- Flat rate: every piece takes the same number of ticks, default 10.
- Or scale it: a percentage of the vanilla duration, so heavy armour still takes longer than a shirt.
- Applies to both wearing and taking off.
- Can be switched off to leave vanilla speeds alone.

## Hiding apparel

- Choose when apparel is hidden: never, while not drafted, or while indoors or not drafted.
- Tick exactly which apparel layers get hidden, including layers added by other mods.
- Always shown while drafted, so you see the armour when it matters.
- Pawns update as they walk through a door or get drafted, no need to reselect them.
- Ctrl+J on a selected pawn marks that hat as always visible, for the ones you want to keep on.
- Apparel that replaces the whole head, like VFE Pirates warcasket helmets, is never hidden.

## Hair under hats

- Hair is drawn under headgear instead of being skipped.
- Hat scaling grows hats slightly so they still cover the hair underneath, with separate handling for normal and narrow heads.
- When a full head helmet is hidden the beard and eyes come back with the hair.
- Ctrl+H on a selected pawn hides that hairstyle under hats, for the ones that clip.
- Ctrl+K hides a specific hair and hat combination, if only one hat is the problem.
